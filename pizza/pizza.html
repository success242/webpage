Our story

De Livvi started as a small stall along Lasu Road, built on one idea: pizza doesn't need to travel across town to reach you. Everything is stretched, sauced and fired the same day, a few steps from where you're sitting.

Whether you're stopping in after work or calling ahead for a family night in, the goal is the same — hot food, fast, without cutting corners on what goes into it.
